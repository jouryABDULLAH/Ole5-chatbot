**Saudi voice: before and after**

The same 51 customer messages, shown in the same order for both versions. Before is the original recorded audit; After is a new live run after the prompt edits. The model and response temperature are unchanged. Full conversations develop independently, so workflow states and generated mock ticket numbers may differ. Knowledge is fixed/stubbed and tickets are mocked; this does not test production retrieval. These are recorded replies, not rewritten demonstrations. One sample per version does not establish a statistical improvement.

Courtesy, cancellations, clarification handling and acknowledging a second problem improved in this sample. The result is still mixed: several technical replies remain formal, ticket lookup has awkward wording, and the delivery overview is less complete. Every original and final reply is shown below, including these weaknesses. This is a qualitative review, not a numerical Saudi-authenticity score.
